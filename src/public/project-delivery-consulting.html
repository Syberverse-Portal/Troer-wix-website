What Troer's Project Support Covers

From structured project delivery and programme coordination through to supplier management and project recovery, Troer provides hands-on support that keeps projects moving.

Project Delivery & Oversight

Structured project management, milestone tracking, risk and issue management, stakeholder coordination and delivery accountability.

Programme Coordination

Multi-project coordination, workstream integration, resource planning and programme-level reporting and accountability.

Supplier & Contractor Management

Contractor oversight, supplier coordination, payment management, compliance verification and performance accountability.

Procurement-Led Project Support

Integration of procurement into project delivery, supplier selection, contract management and procurement control during projects.

Project Recovery & Intervention

Diagnostic assessment of slipping projects, root cause analysis, corrective action planning and hands-on project recovery.

Project Controls & Risk Management

Project controls frameworks, risk and issue registers, mitigation planning and control enforcement across projects.

Stakeholder & Communication Management

Stakeholder engagement plans, communication strategies, escalation management and stakeholder alignment during projects.

Additional Project Capacity

Temporary project management capacity, project support roles and additional project oversight when internal teams need support.

Why Choose Troer for Project Delivery Support

Project problems rarely come from one dramatic event. They build through missed decisions, supplier delays, weak ownership and unresolved issues. Troer brings structure, accountability and delivery focus back into projects.

Flexible Engagement Models

Senior-Level Involvement

Proven in High-Stakes Environments
