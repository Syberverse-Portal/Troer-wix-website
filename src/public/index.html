That is why Troer looks beyond the immediate symptom. We connect Procurement, Supply Chain and Project Delivery to understand what is happening, what is causing it and what needs to happen next. Then we help get it done.

Procurement, Supply Chain & Project Support FAQs

What does a procurement consultancy do?

A procurement consultancy can help organisations improve how they source, select, negotiate with and manage suppliers. Troer goes further by providing hands-on implementation support rather than limiting the engagement to recommendations.

Can Troer work alongside our existing procurement team?

Yes. Troer can provide additional procurement capacity alongside an internal team, take ownership of a specific sourcing exercise or support a wider procurement improvement programme.

Do you offer outsourced procurement?

Yes. Troer can support defined procurement activities or provide ongoing outsourced procurement capability depending on the organisation's requirements.

Can you help us find new suppliers?

Yes. Supplier sourcing can include market research, supplier identification, initial engagement, qualification, commercial comparison, negotiation and implementation support.

Do you support international sourcing?

Yes. Troer can support domestic and cross-border sourcing requirements and help organisations identify, assess and coordinate suppliers across international markets.

Can you help with an urgent supplier problem?

Yes. Troer offers focused procurement and supply-chain intervention for urgent situations including supplier issues, difficult sourcing requirements and additional short-term capacity.

Are you purely an advisory consultancy?

No. Implementation is central to the Troer model. We can help identify the solution and remain involved while the agreed changes are implemented.

Can Troer support a short project rather than a long consultancy engagement?

Yes. Support can be ad-hoc, project-based, ongoing or structured around additional temporary capacity.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Many procurement, supplier, supply-chain and project activities can be supported remotely. On-site support can also form part of an engagement where appropriate.

Where does Troer operate?

Troer is UK-based and can support organisations with UK and international procurement, supply-chain and project requirements.
