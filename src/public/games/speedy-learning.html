Pick a subject

How to play

  1. Pick a subject, then pick a deck.
  2. Look at the card and say your answer out loud.
  3. Tap the card to see if you were right.
  4. Tap again for the next card.

In Spelling Bee, listen to the word and type it in, then press Check. You get 3 tries for each word. Tap 🔊 to hear it again.

In Story Time, watch the picture and read along as the words light up. Swipe or press ▶ for the next page, or press 🔊 Read to me to hear it. Book Ideas shows great books to look out for at the library.

In the subject quizzes (Science, History, Geography and more), tap the answer you think is right.

In Maths, type your answer and press Check. You get 3 tries for each question.
